Who We Are

Growing Agriculture.
Creating Opportunity.

  • Sustainable cultivation across our plantation network
  • Structured investment opportunities tied to real projects

  • Export potential connecting Sri Lankan crops to global markets

FAQ

Common Questions

Still have a question? Our investment advisory team is happy to help.

Talk to an Advisor →
What is plantation investment?+

A structured investment linked directly to a cultivation project — you invest in a defined plan tied to a specific crop and plantation.

How does the investment process work?+

You choose a plan, submit your details, complete documentation, and our team begins cultivation on your behalf.

Can I visit the plantation?+

Yes — plantation visits can be arranged with our team. Contact us to schedule a visit.

How are plantation updates provided?+

Through the Investor Portal, including progress updates, gallery images and harvest reporting.

What happens during harvest?+

Crops are harvested, processed and prepared for either local sale or export, per your plan's return structure.
